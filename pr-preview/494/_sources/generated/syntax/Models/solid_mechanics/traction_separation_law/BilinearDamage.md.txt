# BilinearDamage

**Source:** `models/solid_mechanics/traction_separation_law/BilinearDamage.py`

Bilinear trial damage as a function of effective separation:
$d_\text{trial} = 0$ for $\delta_m \le \delta_c$,
$d_\text{trial} = \frac{\delta_f(\delta_m - \delta_c)}{\delta_m(\delta_f - \delta_c)}$
for $\delta_c < \delta_m < \delta_f$, and $1$ for $\delta_m \ge \delta_f$.

## Inputs

`effective_separation` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Effective separation


## Outputs

`trial_damage` &mdash; `output` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Rate-independent trial damage


## Parameters

`critical_separation` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Critical (damage-onset) separation. May be wired to an upstream `CamanhoDavilaCriticalSeparation` (promotion-capable).

`full_separation` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Full (failure) separation. May be wired to an upstream `BenzeggaghKenaneFullSeparation` or `PowerLawFullSeparation` (promotion-capable).
