# CavityNucleationRate

**Source:** `models/solid_mechanics/traction_separation_law/CavityNucleationRate.py`

Continuous, stress-driven nucleation of grain boundary cavities,

$$
\dot{N} = F_N \left\langle \frac{T_n}{\Sigma_0} \right\rangle^{\beta}
          \dot{\varepsilon},
$$

with $F_N$ the nucleation coefficient, $\Sigma_0$ a reference stress and
$\beta$ the stress exponent. The Macaulay bracket switches nucleation off
under compression.

$\dot{\varepsilon}$ is an ordinary input, so it may be fulfilled either by
`SlidingDrivenStrainRate` (the local
surrogate) or by a true bulk strain rate supplied from the input file.

Leaving this model out of a composition and wiring the damage law without
a ``cavity_density_rate`` gives the same model without nucleation, with
$N$ held at its initial value.

## Inputs

`normal_traction` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Normal traction across the interface

`strain_rate` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Strain rate driving nucleation


## Outputs

`cavity_density_rate` &mdash; `output` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Rate of change of the cavity number density


## Parameters

`nucleation_coefficient` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Nucleation coefficient F_N

`reference_stress` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Nucleation reference stress Sigma_0

`exponent` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Nucleation stress exponent beta
