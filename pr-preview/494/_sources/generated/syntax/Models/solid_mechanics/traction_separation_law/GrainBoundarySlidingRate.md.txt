# GrainBoundarySlidingRate

**Source:** `models/solid_mechanics/traction_separation_law/GrainBoundarySlidingRate.py`

Linear-viscous grain boundary sliding, softened by the cavitated area
fraction,

$$
\dot{[\![u]\!]}_t^v = \frac{T_t}{(1 - f^*)\, \eta}.
$$

$\eta$ is the grain boundary sliding viscosity and $(1 - f^*)$ is the
uncavitated ligament fraction, so the boundary slides ever more freely as
cavities coalesce. The factor vanishes at $f^* = 1$; keeping the effective
fraction strictly below one (see
`EffectiveCavityFraction`) is what keeps
this rate finite.

This is the dashpot half of the sliding branch. The elastic half lives in
`ShamNeedlemanTraction`, which places the
two in series.

## Inputs

`shear_traction` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Shear traction across the interface

`effective_cavity_fraction` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Effective cavitated area fraction softening the sliding viscosity


## Outputs

`viscous_sliding_rate` &mdash; `output` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Rate of the viscous part of the sliding jump


## Parameters

`sliding_viscosity` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Grain boundary sliding viscosity
