# PrecipitateStrength

**Source:** `models/solid_mechanics/crystal_plasticity/PrecipitateStrength.py`

Strength contribution of a precipitate population, defined by
$\tau = \alpha \dfrac{\mu b}{2 R N}$
where $R$ is the mean precipitate radius, $N$ is the precipitate
number density, $\alpha$ is an interaction coefficient, $\mu$ is the
shear modulus and $b$ is the Burgers vector.

The output is a single strength contribution, intended to be combined with
the other contributions through a ``ScalarLinearCombination``.

## Inputs

`precipitate_radius` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Mean precipitate radius

`precipitate_density` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Precipitate number density


## Outputs

`precipitate_strength` &mdash; `output` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: The precipitate strength contribution


## Parameters

`alpha` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Interaction coefficient

`shear_modulus` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Shear modulus

`burgers_vector` &mdash; `parameter` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Magnitude of the Burgers vector
