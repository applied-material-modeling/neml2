# CavityNucleationDamageRate

**Source:** `models/solid_mechanics/traction_separation_law/CavityNucleationDamageRate.py`

The part of $\dot f$ carried by newly nucleated cavities,

$$
\dot{f}_\text{nucleation} = \frac{f}{N} \dot{N}.
$$

New cavities arrive at the current mean size, so the area fraction grows
in proportion to the density.

Split from
`CavityGrowthDamageRate` so that a
nucleation-free variant of the model is assembled by *omitting* leaves
rather than by toggling an option: drop this model, the
`CavityNucleationRate`, and the
`ScalarLinearCombination` that sums the two contributions, hold $N$
constant, and the remaining composition is the pure cavity-growth model.

## Inputs

`cavity_fraction` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Physical cavitated area fraction

`cavity_density` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Cavity number density per unit boundary area

`cavity_density_rate` &mdash; `input` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Rate of change of the cavity number density


## Outputs

`cavity_fraction_nucleation_rate` &mdash; `output` &middot; `Scalar` &middot; **required**
: Area fraction rate contributed by newly nucleated cavities
